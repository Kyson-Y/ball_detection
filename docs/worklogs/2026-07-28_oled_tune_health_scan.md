  character behavior.
- HEALTH long-OK starts a 1.5-second read-only ZDT discovery. No enable,
  speed, position, or motion frame is generated by the diagnostic scan.

- ZDT1 is `NA` in the formal build because UART2 PB15/PB16 is reserved for
  ESP32. ZDT2 is UART3 PB2/PB3.
